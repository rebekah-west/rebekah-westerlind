About

I'm a technical builder with full-stack product experience: I like owning a problem from the first whiteboard sketch to the system customers rely on. Most of my work has lived where data, evaluation and AI workflows meet, and on making complex systems feel simple to the people using them.

At Laude Institute I lead product on the Impact Platform, the system behind Laude's impact profiles. It tracks impactful researchers, papers and open-source projects, and scores their outcomes so Laude can find and back the work that matters most.

I care about clear communication, mentorship and teams that ship. Outside work you'll find me trail running, playing soccer, playing the cornet, reading, or in a Swedish bastu.
